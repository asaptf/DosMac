# DosMac privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026.

DosMac runs retro software locally. The DosMac project does not operate an app analytics, advertising, account, or cloud-storage service. The application does not collect or send your guest disks, documents, product keys, or usage history to the project maintainers. There is no tracking or advertising SDK.

## Local storage

Your library, settings, imported installation images, virtual hard disks, recovery copies, and session logs are stored on your device. A sandboxed Mac edition uses its private application container. The developer edition uses its Application Support directory. Locally stored settings use the app's own UserDefaults.

Installation keys are entered locally. A Windows installation answer file may contain the key inside your private computer. Treat that computer and its backups as private. Do not attach them to public support requests.

## File sharing and guest software

Importing a file copies it into the selected computer. A live shared folder exposes that computer's designated Shared folder to its guest through an app-private service. Exchange images expose only the files you place in them. Do not share files that you do not want guest software to access.

Guest operating systems and applications have their own behavior and privacy practices. DosMac does not control the contents of imported software. Network access depends on the edition and computer configuration; the currently managed live-sharing connection is restricted to the private host relay.

## External services

The App Store preparation edition imports local images and does not offer unreviewed archive downloads or native engine downloads. The developer edition can contact WinWorld, Internet Archive, GitHub, and download mirrors when you explicitly search for or download software or prepare an engine. Such services receive ordinary network request information, including your IP address and the requested search or file, under their own policies. Opening support, documentation, or source links in your browser also contacts the linked service.

Apple may provide its own diagnostics or crash reporting according to your system settings and Apple's privacy policy. Those platform services are separate from DosMac telemetry.

## Control and support

You control the files you import, share, export, and back up. Uninstalling an app may leave local application data; keep copies of wanted documents and remove unwanted data through your operating system's normal file-management tools. Avoid deleting a disk while its computer is running.

For questions, use [DosMac support](support.md). GitHub issues are public and governed by GitHub's privacy policy; include only information you intend to publish. Never post product keys, passwords, personal documents, or guest disks.

This policy describes the repository's current behavior. Store privacy answers and dependency API declarations must be checked against the exact binary submitted for distribution.

A familiar way back

Your own retro computer.

Complete PC emulation gives each computer its own operating system, disks and display.

  1. 01

    Create a computer

    Give your classic environment a place in Library. Start with the included original PC hardware check, without an OS download or product key.

  2. 02

    Bring your software

    Import your floppy and CD images. Use the disk controls during installation. Guided Windows 9x setup is experimental.

  3. 03

    Keep your files close

    Use a designated live shared folder in compatible Windows guests, or an exchange floppy for DOS and Windows 3.1.
